Economists offered more than 1,000 college students real money to quit TikTok for four weeks. On their own, students had to be paid $55 to do it.

When everyone at their university would quit too, the same students offered to pay $24 to make it happen.

Six in ten would be better off if it didn’t exist. They stay because everyone else is there.
